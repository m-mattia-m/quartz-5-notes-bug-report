[Quartz](https://github.com/jackyzha0/quartz) is a system for publishing an Obsidian vault to the internet. I used [bfahrenfort’s Quartz template](https://github.com/bfahrenfort/quartz) as inspiration but made a few custom changes — for example, I adjusted the toggle behavior in the explorer on the left side and modified the order of the components on the right side.

## Usage

You can start quartz with: `npx quartz build --serve`
