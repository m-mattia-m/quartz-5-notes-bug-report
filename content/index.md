# 👋 Welcome

Hi, my name is Mattia. I’m a software engineer based near St. Gallen, Switzerland. My passion is technology — especially coding and constantly learning new things about it. In my spare time, I work on several projects, some of which are open source and available on my GitHub. You can also check out my website for more information about me.

On this site, I collect my knowledge and notes about almost everything I’m interested in and have explored. Most [[Tech]]-related and general content is written in English, but some sections, such as school-related pages, are in German. The reason is simple — Swiss German is my mother tongue, and some of the content was written for school or shared with friends who prefer German.

I’ve been a huge Notion fan for a long time — and I still am — but Notion works best when friends, colleagues, or teammates use it as well. Sometimes it’s hard to convince others to join, and it can also be a bit too flexible, which makes it easy to lose time customizing things. What I really like about this new setup, though, is that my knowledge base is fully versioned with Git — giving me a clear history of my notes and changes over time.

You can also find me on other platforms like [GitHub/m-mattia-m](https://github.com/m-mattia-m) or visit my [personal site](https://mattiamueggler.ch). Feel free to contact me at <hi@mattiamueggler.ch>. If you prefer encrypted communication, we can use a certificate.

**PS:** This site is mainly for myself — to document what I’ve learned and to easily search for examples, structures, and references. Please don’t take everything here as absolute truth; it’s simply my personal collection of knowledge and experiments.
